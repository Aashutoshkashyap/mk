## 3. Services (`/services`)

Six service lines, each with description + scope of works:

### 01 — Roads & Highways
National highways, district roads, rural feeders, and urban arterials. Asphalt, DBST, gravel, and rigid pavement.
*Scope:* Earthwork & sub-grade · Pavement layers · Drainage & culverts · Road furniture

### 02 — Bridges & Structures
Steel-truss, RCC, and pre-stressed bridges across rivers and gorges. Span ranges from 20m to 200m+.
*Scope:* Pier & abutment · Girder fabrication · Deck & approach · Bearings & expansion

### 03 — River Training
Hydraulic protection works for flood mitigation and bank stabilization in monsoon-fed river systems.
*Scope:* Gabion & RCC spurs · Embankment construction · Toe protection · Channelization

### 04 — Buildings
Institutional, commercial, and residential structures executed to Nepal Building Code with seismic detailing.
*Scope:* Structural frame · MEP coordination · Finishes & joinery · Site development

### 05 — Hydropower Civil
Civil packages for run-of-river hydropower including headworks, tunnels, surge tanks, and powerhouse.
*Scope:* Diversion structures · Tunnel & adit · Penstock alignment · Powerhouse civil

### 06 — Water & Sanitation
Municipal water supply, distribution networks, reservoirs, and sewerage infrastructure.
*Scope:* Transmission mains · Reservoirs & tanks · Distribution networks · Treatment plants
