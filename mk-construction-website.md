# MK Construction Company Pvt. Ltd. — Website

A multi-page corporate website for **MK Construction Company Pvt. Ltd.**, a Class-A licensed Nepali infrastructure contractor delivering roads, bridges, river training, buildings, hydropower civil works, and water & sanitation projects across Nepal since 2018.

## Pages

| Page | Route | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| Home | `/` | Hero, client marquee, key metrics, service verticals, featured projects, delivery model, CTA |
| About | `/about` | Company story, mission, values, milestones |
| Services | `/services` | Six engineering verticals in detail |
| Projects | `/projects` | Project portfolio with values, locations, and status |
| Capabilities | `/capabilities` | Plant & equipment, QA/QC, certifications |
| Leadership | `/leadership` | Executive team profiles |
| News | `/news` | News & insights |

| Contact | `/contact` | RFQ / inquiry form and office details |

## Company Positioning

> MK Construction is a professional engineering and infrastructure organization capable of delivering large-scale roads, bridges, river training, buildings, and civil construction projects across Nepal with technical excellence, transparency, and reliability.

### Key Metrics

- **120+** projects delivered
- **₨ 18B** works executed
- **850+** engineers & crew
- **32** districts reached

### Service Verticals

1. Roads & Highways — national highways, district roads, rural feeder networks
2. Bridges & Structures — steel, RCC, and pre-stressed long-span crossings
3. River Training — embankments, spurs, hydraulic protection works
4. Buildings — institutional, commercial, residential (NBC standards)
5. Hydropower Civil — headworks, tunnels, surge tanks, powerhouse packages
6. Water & Sanitation — pipelines, reservoirs, municipal infrastructure

## Design System

- **Style:** Light, editorial, engineering-grade corporate aesthetic
- **Palette:** Warm paper background, deep ink typography, teal/mint accents, hairline rules
- **Typography:** Display serif/sans pairing with uppercase tracked eyebrow labels
- **Motifs:** Blueprint hairline grids, coordinate badges, numbered section indices
- **Motion:** CSS-only reveals, marquee, hover zoom on project imagery

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** TanStack Start v1 (React 19, SSR) with Vite 7
- **Routing:** TanStack Router (file-based routes in `src/routes/`)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with semantic design tokens in `src/styles.css`
- **Icons:** Lucide
- **Components:** shadcn-style UI primitives, shared `SiteLayout` (Nav + Footer)
